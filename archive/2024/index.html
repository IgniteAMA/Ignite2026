Keeping AI Real in CX

Friday, April 19, 2024 · National Underground Railroad Freedom Center, Cincinnati

The in-person IGNITE! 2024 conference brought together local, regional, and national thought leaders and learners to discuss the dynamic intersection of marketing, customer experience (CX), and artificial intelligence (AI).

Attendees got a first-row seat on the latest innovative strategies, case studies, and evolving technologies — hearing from CX and AI experts and enthusiasts with visionary insights for now and for the future, leaving with actionable strategies to apply in their own organizations.

Admission also gave attendees access to the National Underground Railroad Freedom Center's permanent and traveling exhibits, learning stories of freedom's heroes through interactive experiences and storytelling on social justice.
