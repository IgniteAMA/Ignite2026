Who Is Responsible for Customer Experience?

September 20–21, 2018 · Cintas Center, Cincinnati

There may be a person or team with CX in their title, but across the organization, few individuals and departments lack the ability to influence their customers' experience, for better or worse. From first impressions across all the "moments of truth" that make up customer lifetime value, capitalizing on CX opportunities requires a mindful approach to both strategy and execution, access to the right insights, and creative, open lines of communication — internally and externally.

With several iconic keynote speakers, deep-dive track sessions and workshops, peer networking, and a dynamic, interactive Experience Lounge, attendees walked away inspired, empowered, and ready to make an immediate impact on their customers' experience.
